# ISO & Pharmacopoeia Standards Reference

## Essential-oil standards
- **ISO 4720:** Essential oils — Nomenclature (general vocabulary).
- **ISO 4731:** Essential oils — Specifications for individual oils.
- **ISO 11024:** General guidance on chromatographic profiles.
- **ISO 9235:** Aromatic natural raw materials — vocabulary.

## Pharmacopoeia identity & limits
- **PhEur (European Pharmacopoeia):** monographs per oil (e.g. Lavender oil,
  Peppermint oil) with refractive index, specific gravity, optical rotation,
  GC profile and limits for key markers.
- **USP:** complementary identity / assay monographs.

## Adulteration detection (standard checks)
1. GC-MS chemotype match against reference profile.
2. Specific gravity + refractive index within monograph range.
3. Optical rotation within monograph range.
4. Chiral GC for enantiomeric ratio (e.g. linalool enantiomers).
5. Isotope-ratio MS for provenance / synthetic detection.

## Safety limits
- Max residue solvents (for SFE: CO2 trace only; for solvent extraction:
  pharmacopoeia residual-solvent limits ICH Q3C).
- Oxidation markers (e.g. menthofuran in peppermint) within limits.

## Citation discipline
Always cite the specific ISO/PhEur/USP monograph number and revision year in
the output. When a monograph is unavailable, flag as a limitation (gate G4).
